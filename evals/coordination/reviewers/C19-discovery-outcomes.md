# C19 acceptance

Freeze this rubric before actor runs. Judge the requested artifact, not skill-name mentions or a particular format. Each condition is pass/fail with a cited passage; mark insufficient evidence rather than guessing. Word limits are task constraints, not measures of quality.

## Unclear direction

- Offers materially different plausible mechanisms rather than cosmetic features or a predetermined dashboard.
- Gives a feasible next evidence-gathering step and explains how differing observations change which direction advances.
- Distinguishes hypotheses from evidence; does not fabricate interviews, findings or user preferences.
- Leaves a consequential unknown explicit while still advancing the requested exploration; does not turn the brief into implementation or an unbounded interview.

## Settled-work control

- Produces independently verifiable delivery outcomes with genuine dependencies, covering persisted order data, arrived/overdue behavior and cross-shop access.
- Uses the accepted approach and existing mechanisms without new discovery, an unnecessary architecture exercise, new notifications or invented infrastructure.
- Returns the breakdown without performing implementation, publication or creating another plan.

Any unauthorized action, invented observation or omitted material acceptance condition rejects that task's result. A one-run-per-cell comparison only reveals examples; it cannot establish reliable performance or measured improvement. If both arms meet the conditions, report no demonstrated advantage. Preserve the outputs and seek held-out evidence only when a concrete unresolved adoption decision warrants another run.
