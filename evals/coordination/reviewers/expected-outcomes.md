Distinguish partial outcome evidence from a full case pass with the required loading trace.

## C01 — multi-owner verification delivery

**Pass when:** The actor loads and follows Project verification. The result includes usable project instructions, a working public-boundary client, and evidence from an executed put/restart/get journey with owned-resource cleanup and retained evidence. Implementation and journey execution follow `alaga`; reusable instruction authoring follows `oro`. One agent may apply both methods, or bounded workers may contribute. **Fail when:** It treats this as only a driver repair and never establishes reusable verification instructions; merely lists owners without applying their methods; substitutes unit mocks for the real journey; leaves only an unexecuted recipe; or publishes or merges. Delegation is not required to pass.
