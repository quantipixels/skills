The earlier 91 tests are mechanical tests, not model trials: 70 runtime, 15 native-package, three session-evidence and three HTML tests, plus other exit-status gates. Earlier read-only setup and memo-destination probes remain separate observations.

Mirror-only adaptations are excluded from this official comparison.

This follow-up has adaptive observations across instruction revisions, but no preregistered comparative threshold, direct-owner or competitor control, validated blind judge, complete trace capture or cost measurement. It establishes the listed capability observations, including the ambiguous-fixture caveat; it cannot establish that the new wording caused improvement or that Alárinà outperforms CE/PStack.
