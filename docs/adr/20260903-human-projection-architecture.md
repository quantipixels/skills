# Normalize semantic authority and denormalize human understanding

Status: Accepted

QP keeps semantic truth with exact owner records/results while allowing derived human projections to intentionally colocate concise decision-critical meaning from several owners. A canonical record remains authoritative; a human view exists to make the current state, evidence strength, limiting conditions, and next action understandable without record archaeology.

## Human visibility

A link to canonical detail is sufficient for provenance but not for visibility when omission could materially change a human's judgment of outcome/scope, a current progression gate, decision/contract sufficiency, architecture/design conditions, delivery state, proof, risk/recovery, freshness, or next action.

Atọ́nà owns initiative-level human-visibility obligations for a maintained Initiative Assurance View. `html-artifact` materializes those obligations as a denormalized read model and cannot originate the underlying status, verdict, confidence, readiness, decision, or recommendation.

Human-critical meaning is visible with canonical provenance; exhaustive packets remain at their owners. This is deliberate denormalization for reading, not semantic duplication.

## Viewpoints and composition

A projection starts from the reader/stakeholder, current concern, governing judgment/action, and evidence cut. Use one primary viewpoint by default; add another only when a materially different concern would otherwise distort the main view.

Source sections do not define presentation sections. HTML chooses representation per material relationship. Multiple records may collapse into one traceability/assurance view, while one source section may use several representations.

For living initiatives, after the first complete view foreground material delta: changed/reopened/stale/closed claims, new evidence/candidate, changed gate, and next action.

## Evaluation semantics

QP keeps these concepts distinct:

- verdict — what current evidence establishes;
- confidence — strength/limits of that evidence or judgment;
- comparative grade/score — performance against an explicit rubric for viable alternatives;
- gate — mandatory condition that cannot be averaged away;
- readiness — whether progression is justified;
- disposition — action following evaluation; and
- evidence gap — missing proof capable of changing the result.

Do not create a universal health score. Apply hard gates before comparative rubrics and foreground the weakest material claim when it controls progression.

## Representation

Coverage comes before representation minimization. “Smallest sufficient view” means the least machinery that preserves all meaning the reader must see, not the fewest visible facts.

HTML Artifact separates representation, renderer/capability, and delivery. Strategic mature external capabilities are expert knowledge when their durable problem fit reduces rediscovery or bespoke rendering risk. Named anchors are non-exclusive; unsupported information shapes remain open to current capability discovery rather than degrading to prose or a cached set.

Dependency/runtime policy governs trust, delivery, fallback, reproducibility, data access, and cost after representational value is understood. A focused dependency can simplify the total system when it replaces bespoke machinery with a mature domain grammar.

## Projection trust

Cross-record projections declare a coherent evidence cut; stale/partial dependencies cannot silently support current claims. Human-critical obligations maintain a coverage map from source meaning to visible placement/provenance. Bidirectional traceability may expose requirement, implementation, or proof orphans when the owning semantic evidence establishes those gaps.

Presentation strength never increases epistemic authority: observed, inferred, proposed, and illustrative content remain distinguishable where ambiguity matters.

## Rejected alternatives

QP will not merge specialist records back into one Atọ́nà Markdown, treat source links as sufficient for all human-critical meaning, force one Markdown section to one visualization, create a public rubric/show-me owner merely for these methods, maintain a closed renderer allowlist, or reject mature external capabilities merely because native code could reproduce them.

## Consequence

QP optimizes semantic records for correctness/composition, human projections for conscious judgment, and implementation mechanisms for representational leverage. Those surfaces may therefore use different degrees of normalization and different kinds of minimalism without competing for authority.
