# Reset the public Design surface around independent outcomes

Status: Proposed

QP will stop treating Design as a stable taxonomy of surface-specific public owners. The portfolio keeps only design outcomes that currently justify independent selection, and moves implementation mechanics behind the delivery owner that already owns software changes.

## Decision

- Fold application UI implementation, component-library selection inside settled architecture, rendered UI proof, and reusable token/component realization into Alága as conditional `ui-delivery` depth.
- Remove the public `asa-oju-ibanisoro` and `eto-apere` owners after preserving their behavior-bearing guidance behind Alága. Do not carry Ètò Àpẹrẹ's fallback token compiler forward; prefer project-native/current focused realization.
- Retire `apere`: design-only routing duplicates Alárinà's general routing contract and does not own an independent artifact, authority, or acceptance boundary.
- Retire `banner-design` and `social-graphics`: their public distinction is primarily a surface taxonomy. Use ordinary host/image/design capability plus current target specifications unless another QP owner such as Brand materially governs the result.
- Move `slides` to Productivity because a presentation is an independently useful communication artifact, not merely a Design subdomain.
- Move `amoye-ui-ux` and `brand` to Experimental without changing their semantic ownership. Both retain independently useful judgment/identity outcomes, but their long-term public topology should be proven through real eligible work before stable Design taxonomy is rebuilt.
- Keep `prototype` Experimental as a disposable decision instrument.

## Boundaries

Experimental remains a maturity group, not a blanket invocation ban. Amọ̀ye and Brand remain available under their normal triggers and authority boundaries.

Alága does not become the owner of design judgment or brand identity. It consumes confirmed direction/identity when those independent results exist and owns only implementation, exact candidate identity, native proof, and convergence.

Do not replace the retired graphic owners with a generic `design` super-skill. When no remaining QP owner materially improves a graphic-design request, return to ordinary host/domain capability rather than force routing through the nearest skill.

## Why

The previous surface encoded several operational distinctions as public semantic owners:

- design-specific routing (`apere`);
- UI implementation (`asa-oju-ibanisoro`);
- token/component realization (`eto-apere`); and
- banner vs social surface taxonomy.

Those branches can be carried more coherently as owner-local depth or ordinary capability. Keeping them public increases selection/routing burden without adding independent authority or durable results.

Amọ̀ye, Brand, Slides, and Prototype survive because they produce materially different outcomes: interface judgment, durable identity meaning, presentation artifacts, and disposable decision instruments respectively.

## Revisit

Promote or extract a Design owner only when repeated eligible work proves an independently useful result/authority boundary whose direct public selection is materially simpler than keeping the capability behind Alága, a current experimental owner, or ordinary host capability.
