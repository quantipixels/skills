# Postmortem

Use when a finished or materially paused event, incident, session, or bounded corpus needs evidence-backed reconstruction and justified improvements. Active recovery uses Incident recovery first.

1. Choose by result: [ayewo-igba-ise](../commands/ayewo-igba-ise.md) reconstructs an event; [ayewo-retro](../commands/ayewo-retro.md) improves the agent's working environment; [ayewo-corpus](../commands/ayewo-corpus.md) assesses cross-session patterns, skill use or reusable artifact lessons. Give the selected owner the boundary, expected result, exact candidate/environment and available evidence. Do not run all three by default.
2. Use [alaga-diagnose](../commands/alaga-diagnose.md) only when an unresolved causal mechanism materially changes the postmortem. Keep missing, sampled, truncated, or inaccessible evidence visible.
3. Separate one-off execution mistakes from durable friction in ownership, sequencing, evidence, tools, authority, context, or workflow shape. Preserve effective mechanisms as well as failures.
4. After judgment, the shared retrospective method may retain a qualified custom workflow within write scope. Stop other work at the postmortem and owned recommendations unless remediation was also requested. For authorized remediation, route the supported change through [alaga-deliver](../commands/alaga-deliver.md), [oro-sigidi](../commands/oro-sigidi.md) or [oro-eniyan](../commands/oro-eniyan.md), [amose](../commands/amose.md), or the applicable owner.

Finish with the material reconstruction, recovery cost, effective actions, ranked friction, earned or rejected lessons, owners, and remaining uncertainty.
