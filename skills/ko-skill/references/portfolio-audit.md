# Portfolio audit

Use for an explicitly bounded read-only portfolio review. Do not edit, install, promote, or publish from an audit request alone.

Establish the requested inventory and relevant package/router surfaces. Distinguish source, installed, active, and published state only when the question depends on them.

## Find consequential problems

Apply the main skill's identity and behavior criteria to the requested set. Look for competing outcomes, taxonomy-driven splits, mis-triggered skills, copied callee workflows, and handoffs that add no independent result or authority boundary.

Check representative end-to-end task paths, including loaded references and delegated work. A short entry point can conceal expensive coordination. Check cumulative pressure from instructions such as “smallest,” “minimum,” and “concise”: together they may reward compressed code, omitted proof, or lost explanation. Verify that the readability, evidence, and scope safeguards actually load on the same execution path, including worker assignments; a safeguard in an unloaded reference does not counter that pressure. Keep deliberate isolation, useful lightweight steering contracts, and independent safety rules when they protect the result.

When portfolio focus, description overlap, or loading pressure is material, read [instruction economics](instruction-economics.md). Use `../scripts/skill-doctor.py` when its structural inventory can narrow the audit: routing-pointer and entrypoint footprint, resource shape, entrypoint-linked references, unreferenced Markdown candidates, explicit related-skill mentions, and script/test presence. Treat every metric as a lead. Do not infer quality, context cost, routing behavior, or redundancy from size/counts alone.

Distinguish a wrong instruction from missing expertise, ignored/unavailable context, or a host/tool limitation. More prose cannot repair every failure. Examine newly exposed decisions after consolidation: preserve explicit constraints and authority, evidence-based conclusions, and legitimate local discretion; flag consequential guesswork.

Use resource-placement, reference-quality, script-boundary, or instruction-economics guidance only for findings involving those resources. Experimental maturity decisions use [experimental disposition](experimental-disposition.md); source structure alone cannot establish real-use value or frequency.

## Verify and report

Run applicable package/link/metadata checks. Confirm suspected problems against the exact source; use realistic before/after tasks when changed behavior remains uncertain, not keyword assertions for model judgment. Standing model-behavior datasets and judge harnesses belong in the internal evaluation repository rather than this package.

Return prioritized findings, the evidence and consequence of each, the smallest useful action, and material coverage limits. Include healthy patterns or rejected cuts when they prevent a plausible wrong change. Do not produce empty category inventories or a permanent audit document by default. No finding means none within the reviewed scope, not proof of portfolio perfection.
