# Visual Taste

Give the requested surface a deliberate visual character rooted in its content and audience. Counter default composition through concrete design choices, not a different stock aesthetic. This skill owns visual bias correction; the current design, implementation, or artifact owner retains delivery and verification.

## Find the actual default

Read the brief, current surface and available visual references. Preserve supplied brand, content, behavior and scope. A missing design document does not make an existing product a blank canvas. For a new surface, use the material it must communicate rather than inventing a generic landing page first.

Name the specific failure: interchangeable silhouette, weak hierarchy, cosmetic variety, decorative chrome, or expression that obstructs the task. Diagnose the rendered result when available; source-only inspection does not prove appearance. Use [pattern corrections](references/pattern-corrections.md) when the failure is hard to distinguish from a legitimate convention.

A familiar font, card, gradient or symmetrical layout is not a defect by itself. The question is whether the arrangement serves this subject or merely arrived by habit.

## Make a committed choice

Decide what should lead: the work itself, a comparison, a mechanism, a decision, or an action. Let that determine composition, type hierarchy, density and imagery together. Keep the rest subordinate. A portfolio may let the work dominate; a financial operation may express care through precise alignment and restraint.

When the first idea repeats the default, consider a structurally different alternative before polishing it. Changing colors, fonts or corner radii alone is not a new direction. Choose the stronger fit; do not require a variant gallery, random selection, design dials or another approval round for a settled brief.

Use reference material for a specific transferable idea—cropping, scale contrast, rhythm, relationship or feedback—not its entire visual identity. Carry one useful idea fully into the target instead of collecting unrelated effects. Prefer a coherent strong move to making everything louder.

## Correct at the right scale

Change the named surface within existing authority. A section refinement should work within its surrounding design; a requested redesign can replace the composition while preserving product truth and required behavior.

Use real content and available assets. Make a diagram show the relationship, an example demonstrate the idea, and an image carry subject matter. Do not substitute invented metrics, decorative code, pseudo-diagrams or unrelated stock imagery for missing substance. Keep unavailable evidence explicit.

Expression can come from precise typography, strong cropping, useful density, whitespace, alignment, material, or purposeful motion. Neither maximalism nor minimalism is the default cure. Repetition is valuable when it makes comparable items easier to scan. Preserve familiar controls and accessibility; novelty does not justify hidden actions, unreadable type or disrupted reading order.

When constructing or changing an HTML report or explainer, use `html-artifact` for construction, representation mechanics and living-document behavior. Direction-only work returns to its existing owner. For production UI, use the applicable installed implementation skill or project conventions. Do not introduce a framework, component library or animation dependency solely to satisfy this skill.

## Judge the result

Compare the corrected surface with the starting point or likely default on the actual reader task:

- Does the first view direct attention to the right material?
- Would swapping only the name and accent color still make it fit an unrelated subject?
- Did the change improve hierarchy and character beyond cosmetic variation?
- Can the reader still find, understand and operate everything required, including at a narrow width?

Use the delivery owner's render and interaction evidence; inspect the changed visual boundary rather than inventing a separate testing workflow. If rendering is unavailable, identify the remaining visual uncertainty. Stop once the requested correction holds; do not keep polishing for novelty.

Return the applied correction or requested direction, its concrete reason, and material verification limits. Do not report a taste score or claim human preference from your own assessment.
