# Comparable evidence

State the claim: package validity, discovery, selection, loading, task completion, human understanding or incremental benefit. Supplying a skill bypasses discovery; proposing a route is not execution; an uncontrolled success does not establish benefit.

Freeze contract, task, source/candidate identity, tools, permissions, resource boundary and criterion before observing outcomes. For instruction comparisons hold model/effort compatible; for model comparisons hold guidance compatible. Preserve hard constraints. Include a plausible control where guidance should stay inactive or another implementation is correct.

Use realistic tasks. Supply necessary requirements but withhold private judging criteria and known-good patches. Separate folders are not a sandbox. Use native isolation where available; otherwise disclose the limit. Never execute untrusted returned code on a privileged host.

Use independent acceptance and inspect artifacts/effects. Calibrate the oracle with valid and plausible invalid candidates. Grade flexible output by required meaning, not one preferred shape. For human/model judgments use concrete rubrics, accepted/rejected examples and blinded labels/order where feasible. Agreement is not correctness.

Use fresh sessions or a controlled equivalent. Preserve traces for claims about reads/invocations; self-reported names do not prove loading. Keep failed, invalid, blocked and completed attempts, retries, human corrections and unknown telemetry. A replay is not a fresh model run.

Repeat enough to reveal relevant variability when claiming differences. Pair/alternate conditions where useful, avoid contention, and confirm promising results on fresh or held-out tasks. Noisy screens may be inconclusive; do not invent significance or call no observed difference equivalence.

Include cost shifted into workers, tools, retries and human review. Distinguish warmup, steady-state and caches when material. Correctness, authority, compatibility and resource bounds are gates, not averaged penalties.

A checker defect invalidates affected verdicts: retain originals, correct the contract/oracle from independent evidence and recheck affected arms consistently. Rechecking an unchanged artifact is not another model attempt.

Return candidates, conditions, acceptance, comparison, interventions, limits and keep/reject/inconclusive disposition. The host owns execution/isolation; the experiment owns its question/evidence. Keep a useful record, not a universal campaign platform.
