# Fihanmi

Own a useful human view of supplied meaning, including HTML construction when requested. Keep domain conclusions, accepted decisions and authority with the source owner. Use [oro](../oro/SKILL.md) for prose and [amose](../amose/SKILL.md) only for unresolved domain meaning.

## Compose for the reader's job

Reuse the topic, evidence, audience and established design. A short paragraph or conceptual diff can suffice. Do not restart research or create an artifact merely because the user says “show me.” For PRs use native Markdown; [seda-pr](../seda-pr/SKILL.md) owns publication and risk judgment.

Lead with what matters: decision, consequential change, mechanism, comparison or specimen. Put qualifications next to the claims they limit. Show alternatives together. Use canonical terms and exact identifiers; provide only the prerequisites the intended reader needs.

Prefer a small example, interface, state transition or execution path beside its consequence to abstract headings. Choose form from the subject, not a reusable hero-and-cards shell. Familiar structure is useful when it serves comparison; novelty is not a requirement. Remove ornament and metrics that imply unsupported certainty.

Let readers inspect evidence, question assumptions and respond to an exact target/revision. Distinguish source facts, model-derived illustrations and unaccepted feedback. An interactive control does not grant approval or prove execution.

## Construct the right surface

For a portable browser artifact or living plan read [HTML delivery](references/html.md). Keep one current document with stable identity and useful anchors; no equivalent Markdown plan. Existing independent evidence records remain sources, not duplicate plans.

For other surfaces return or apply the smallest adequate presentation directly. Respect the product's design owner and explicit aesthetic direction; do not take over production UI, brand discovery or domain analysis.

## Verify promised utility

Check whether the reader can understand the direction, inspect decisive evidence, compare material alternatives and respond unambiguously. Keep blockers, stale inputs and uncertainty visible. Source inspection supports fidelity; rendered checks support layout/interaction; observed reader use is needed to claim better comprehension or faster review.

Do not confuse artifact size with required testing depth. Stop when the reader's actual task is served, not when every visual pattern has been used.
