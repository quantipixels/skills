# Continuation handoff

Use when another lead/session must resume the work. Keep authoritative artifacts at their existing owners; carry only coordination facts that cannot be safely reconstructed.

> Resume **[outcome]** within **[accepted scope and authority]**.
>
> Current results and exact candidate/workspace identities: **[locators]**.
> Pending assignments, dependencies, and unresolved decisions: **[state]**.
> Verified evidence and anything now stale: **[receipts/locators]**.
> Next useful action and its prerequisites: **[action]**.
>
> Refresh mutable state before relying on it. Reuse valid completed work; do not assume worker handles or background execution survived the previous session.

Preserve relevant isolated `.qp` state with its candidate. This handoff records resumable work; it does not create a persistent worker runtime.
