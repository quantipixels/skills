# Evaluate instructions

Use when a consequential instruction, routing or model choice needs a controlled comparison. Routine edits need no model evaluation.

Start from an observed failure or a concrete behavior at risk. Name the decision the result will change and why source inspection or a deterministic check cannot settle it. Use `ayewo-igba-ise` when real-session evidence needs reconstruction; do not turn every wording change into an evaluation campaign.

Choose examples from actual corrections and contrasting successes where available. Label synthetic cases and their purpose. Include diverse or ordinary cases alongside known failures to expose blind spots; a targeted failure sample cannot estimate real-world prevalence. Keep closely related sessions or variants together when separating development from held-out evidence.

Pin baseline and candidate, representative tasks, hard acceptance constraints, useful improvement threshold and finite budget including confirmation. Freeze workload and judgment method; re-establish affected baselines if they change.

Name the claim: discovery, selection, loading, execution or incremental benefit. Supplying a skill bypasses discovery; a routing answer does not demonstrate execution; success without a control cannot establish benefit.

Vary the intended factor. Hold model, effort, tools, permissions and tasks compatible for instruction comparisons; hold instructions compatible for model comparisons. Use fresh sessions or controlled equivalents through host isolation. Separate directories alone do not prevent history leakage.

Supply realistic tasks and necessary contracts without private judging criteria, preferred solutions or other actors' answers. Include a nearby task where guidance should stay inactive when testing selection.

Inspect artifacts and independent observations. Use host traces for loading or tool-use claims, not self-reports. Inspect actor changes to tests and harnesses that could hide failures. Synthetic transcripts and source checks are not runtime trials; mark unrun trials explicitly.

Record completed, failed, blocked and invalid runs separately. A checker defect invalidates affected verdicts: preserve results, correct it from independent evidence and reassess every affected arm. Rechecking unchanged artifacts is not a fresh model run.

Repeat enough to expose variability. Count tools, retries, corrections and human intervention; missing telemetry is unknown. Reject skipped work, weakened proof and hidden costs. Instruction length does not prove savings. Differences within noise are inconclusive.

Prefer executable acceptance for objective properties. For interpretive judgments, assess one concrete failure at a time against explicit pass/fail conditions and cite decisive output or trace evidence; retain uncertainty when evidence is insufficient. Useful severity or continuous measurements can remain separate from acceptance. Do not substitute a holistic quality score or preferred phrasing for the outcome.

Before relying on a model judge across many runs, compare it with qualified human labels on separate development and held-out examples. Inspect false passes and false failures separately, including ambiguous cases and human disagreement; overall agreement can hide missed failures. Choose tolerances from the decision's consequences and report sample counts and uncertainty, not universal sample quotas or accuracy targets. A small uncalibrated review is advisory evidence, not a validated judge.

Fix rubric and judge configuration, keep calibration examples out of held-out assessment, and blind labels or vary order where feasible. Judge agreement does not prove correctness. Authority failures and unacceptable outcomes are hard rejections. Confirm promising candidates on fresh or held-out tasks; revisit calibration when the judge, task distribution or governing contract changes.

Stop at a supported result, exhausted budget or hypotheses, interruption or material gap. Report candidates, conditions, outcomes, limits and retain/reject/inconclusive conclusion in the existing record. No permanent benchmark is required. Adoption, installation and publication retain their authority requirements; refresh affected comparisons if finalization changes the candidate.

After the decision, retain a case only when it protects a live failure mechanism or unresolved question worth revisiting. Consolidate overlapping proof, retire obsolete obligations and keep historical results at their original evidence cut. A run that cannot change a decision is not justified merely because the case exists.
