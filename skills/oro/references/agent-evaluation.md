# Evaluate instructions

Use when a consequential instruction, routing or model choice needs a controlled comparison. Routine edits need no model evaluation.

Pin baseline and candidate, representative tasks, hard acceptance constraints, useful improvement threshold and finite budget including confirmation. Freeze workload and judgment method; re-establish affected baselines if they change.

Name the claim: discovery, selection, loading, execution or incremental benefit. Supplying a skill bypasses discovery; a routing answer does not demonstrate execution; success without a control cannot establish benefit.

Vary the intended factor. Hold model, effort, tools, permissions and tasks compatible for instruction comparisons; hold instructions compatible for model comparisons. Use fresh sessions or controlled equivalents through host isolation. Separate directories alone do not prevent history leakage.

Supply realistic tasks and necessary contracts without private judging criteria, preferred solutions or other actors' answers. Include a nearby task where guidance should stay inactive when testing selection.

Inspect artifacts and independent observations. Use host traces for loading or tool-use claims, not self-reports. Inspect actor changes to tests and harnesses that could hide failures. Synthetic transcripts and source checks are not runtime trials; mark unrun trials explicitly.

Record completed, failed, blocked and invalid runs separately. A checker defect invalidates affected verdicts: preserve results, correct it from independent evidence and reassess every affected arm. Rechecking unchanged artifacts is not a fresh model run.

Repeat enough to expose variability. Count tools, retries, corrections and human intervention; missing telemetry is unknown. Reject skipped work, weakened proof and hidden costs. Instruction length does not prove savings. Differences within noise are inconclusive.

For scored outputs, fix rubric and judge configuration, calibrate on accepted/rejected examples, and blind labels or vary order where feasible. Judge agreement does not prove correctness. Authority failures and unacceptable outcomes are hard rejections. Confirm promising candidates on fresh or held-out tasks.

Stop at a supported result, exhausted budget or hypotheses, interruption or material gap. Report candidates, conditions, outcomes, limits and retain/reject/inconclusive conclusion in the existing record. No permanent benchmark is required. Adoption, installation and publication retain their authority requirements; refresh affected comparisons if finalization changes the candidate.
