# Human-facing writing

Write for the reader's actual technical familiarity. Preserve facts, quotations, citations, decisions, uncertainty, exact identifiers, authoritative terminology and native format. Establish concepts the reader needs to reason correctly; do not define routine terms or replace precise domain language for style.

Use the artifact's reader job. Diátaxis can separate tutorial, how-to, reference and explanation when useful; reports, specifications, messages and other native forms need not be forced into it. Apply Google developer-documentation style as a lens: direct, active, unambiguous prose; guarded conditions before actions; common case before exceptions; stable terms; descriptive links; parallel lists. Subject terminology and requested voice take precedence.

## Editing and shortening

For supplied prose, change language by effect rather than mechanical banned-word replacement. Remove unsupported promotion, vague attribution, abstract padding, formulaic framing, filler, sycophancy and decorative formatting when they obscure meaning or voice. Keep uncertainty the evidence requires and enough prerequisites, actors, states, units, causality and constraints for the reader to act correctly.

Shorten only when requested. Remove filler and duplicate conclusions first, then identical explanations, unhelpful examples and rationale that changes no decision. Move background only to a reliable linked home. Keep repetition that serves a distinct warning, exception, decision boundary or independent proof. Word count describes the result; it does not prove it.

Compare the revision with its source: facts, citations, identifiers, decisions, required fields, uncertainty and conceptual adequacy must survive unless the task authorizes changing them. Do not impose cleanup on unrelated work.

## Asynchronous stakeholder questionnaire

Use when `arojinle` supplies an unanswered decision frontier whose controlling knowledge belongs to someone not present. Draft a concise questionnaire that states its purpose, essential context, recipient role, priority or response boundary, each answerable question, and how each answer will affect the decision. Preserve unknowns and make assumptions explicit; do not ask the sender to invent the recipient's facts.

Return a send-ready draft plus the unresolved decisions it covers. Drafting does not authorize sending. Replies return to `arojinle` for reconciliation and do not automatically constitute the user's approval.

## Return

For authoring or editing, return complete usable prose first; for review, return findings first. Mention only material unresolved meaning, evidence, authority, audience, technical adequacy or voice.
