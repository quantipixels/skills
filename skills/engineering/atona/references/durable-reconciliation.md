Use `amose` for project/domain knowledge, learnings, exclusions, or decision records. Supply the confirmed knowledge delta, controlling evidence and initiative/result identity, existing destination when known, and write authority. Keep only each required obligation's owner, destination, current result/proof, and blocker in the plan.
