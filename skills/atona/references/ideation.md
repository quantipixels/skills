# Explore directions

Expand one grounded opportunity into a bounded, mechanism-diverse candidate set. Pin the beneficiaries, current reality, constraints, non-goals, evidence boundary, horizon and useful outcome; use `iwadi` or `irinse` only for a material evidence/tool gap.

Generate before judging so an early preference does not collapse the search. Candidates must differ in causal or delivery mechanism, not naming or cosmetics. Include a conservative, structural or surprising direction only when evidence makes it credible; stop when additions would repeat existing mechanisms. `NO_CREDIBLE_DIRECTION` is valid, and no candidate quota applies.

For each candidate that needs downstream identity, record its mechanism, beneficiary effect, supporting evidence/constraint, main cost/dependency/operational burden/failure mode, and decisive assumption. Reject duplicates, weak variants and options whose expected value does not justify their cost, with one concise reason. Rank only when candidates share decision-changing criteria; otherwise group by trade-off.

Return the opportunity and evidence boundary, candidate inventory, rejections, credible survivors, decisive uncertainty, and next decision/action boundary. Exploration creates possibilities, not confirmed decisions or requirements. For an end-to-end initiative, return them to Atọ́nà and continue under existing authority.
