# Durable knowledge reconciliation

Read only when stable governing knowledge needs reconciliation beyond normal initiative artifacts, or an existing durable authority became materially stale. Ordinary implementation rationale remains in the plan/spec/PR/history.

Record the confirmed knowledge change and its evidence as a committed decision or domain note.

Update required user/operator documentation. A maintained normative specification is a behavior contract.

Keep only each required obligation's owner, destination, current result/proof, and blocker in the plan. Do not close while a required source remains stale or its reconciliation is blocked by missing evidence, destination, or authority. Consume the owning result rather than repeating its checks. When no durable obligation exists, omit the section and do not make lack of promotion a closure blocker.
