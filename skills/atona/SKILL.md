# Atọ́nà

Own initiative decisions, progression and combined acceptance. Begin at the first unresolved result; reuse settled choices and sound current evidence. An accepted local change goes directly to [alaga](../alaga/SKILL.md).

## Establish what is wanted

Pin the problem, beneficiary, observable success, scope/non-goals, constraints and requested stop. Research discoverable facts rather than asking the user to supply them. For unsettled purpose, consequential trade-offs or dependent choices, read [decisions](references/decisions.md). Ask one already-understood bounded choice directly. An interview may stop at confirmed understanding without starting delivery.

For a behavior specification or delivery breakdown read [contracts and slices](references/contracts.md) and return that bounded result. Neither needs a separate initiative or HTML plan.

## Keep one usable plan

For initiative planning use [fihanmi](../fihanmi/SKILL.md) and its HTML branch. Reuse the current location; otherwise use `.qp/atona/<subject>/index.html`. Atọ́nà owns meaning. Show the first direction before implementation, then maintain the same document. Chat supplements it; do not create an equivalent Markdown plan.

Keep the outcome, rationale, accepted/proposed/open decisions, dependencies, candidate, progress, evidence and next action visible. Preserve consequential alternatives and counterevidence. A recommendation is not user confirmation. Report a missing presentation capability and continue independent authorized work.

## Shape and execute

Resolve only gaps that change delivery. [Amose](../amose/SKILL.md) owns domain meaning, [architect](../architect/SKILL.md) consequential structure, and [iwadi](../iwadi/SKILL.md) substantive research. Consume their results without imposing every specialist on every initiative.

For consequential or hard-to-reverse work, assume the plan failed. Trace credible causes, safeguards, earliest warning and recovery. Correct unsafe sequencing and missing proof before treating the affected slice as ready. A solo challenge is not independent review. Record material dispositions in this plan, not another risk system.

Deliver dependency-ready, outcome-complete slices through Alága with acceptance, evidence, workspace and authority. Maintain one writer per mutable resource. Missing dependent decisions need not stop independent work. Preserve publication, merge, deployment and destructive-operation boundaries.

Accept integrated behavior, not task counts. Refresh only invalidated decisions and proof. Use [atunwo](../atunwo/SKILL.md) for requested or warranted independent judgment; self-checking is not independent. [Seda PR](../seda-pr/SKILL.md) owns authorized publication.

## Finish at the actual boundary

A build request ends at delivered and verified behavior, not a plan or handoff. Planning ends at a usable plan with open decisions and unexecuted proof visible. State exact blockers rather than inventing acceptance.

For resumption retain source/candidate identity, workspace/branch, accepted decisions, proof locators and next step in the same plan. Keep machine-local paths out of public artifacts. Preserve unreconciled work; deleting a worktree requires authority. Close promptly once acceptance and the human view are current; optional polish does not extend delivery.
