# Word formation and proposed terminology

Use for morphology or a new technical term. Word-formation evidence and project adoption are separate decisions.

Yorùbá nominal formation includes prefixation, compounding and full/partial reduplication, with construction-specific tone, category and meaning. Morphophonological alternations are not string concatenation; verify the whole attested word and its natural sentence use. Analyses disagree on some labels and boundaries, including affixation versus infixing reduplication, so do not generalize one account into a universal rule.

Use attested examples to constrain analogy: Eleshin illustrates `ì-` plus `fẹ́` forming `ìfẹ́` (love as a noun); this does not make the prefix a universal translation of an English suffix. Arokoyo's gerundive examples `tà → títà` (sell → selling) and `lò → lílò` (use → using) copy the initial consonant plus high `í`, rather than repeat the entire word. Her allomorph examples place `alá-` and `elé-` in the `oní-` family and include `oní + ilé → onílé`; they demonstrate conventional formations, not a rule for concatenating arbitrary entries. Verify new formations independently. Pulleyblank's cited publisher abstract supports only the bounded distinction between reduplication categories, not a complete productive paradigm.

For terminology, establish the concept, users and grammatical role, then search attested specialist/general usage. Prefer a fitting established term; otherwise compare a descriptive phrase, supported derivation/compound, semantic extension or borrowing. Explain components, marked pronunciation, sense, evidence and competing meanings. Borrowing is valid when it communicates better than an unfamiliar coinage.

Label unattested forms as proposed. Linguistic plausibility, attestation, frequency, speaker acceptance, project adoption and formal standardization are distinct evidence. Keep the English term when a consequential public coinage remains uncertain; never invent etymology or tones by analogy. Use [glossary maintenance](glossary.md) only for actual term decisions or authorized writes.
