# Source composition

Use for substantial, evidence-heavy, living, reused or owner-record input. HTML is a purpose-shaped read model, not the canonical archive. It may group, deduplicate, order, summarize and represent supplied meaning; it cannot originate domain conclusions, priority, ownership, status, readiness or recommendations.

Pin reader, current judgment/action, governing question, one source-supported thesis, evidence cutoff, coherent source cut, caller visibility obligations, dominant relationships, first-viewport obligation and snapshot/living state. Read owner records before raw evidence and pin their identity/revision/status/candidate. Incompatible controlling revisions stay visibly partial or stale.

For each meaningful unit, choose:

- coverage: complete, partial, input gap or excluded;
- placement: foreground, supporting, evidence, source-only, archive or superseded; and
- fidelity: exact, meaning-preserving, aggregate, representative, pointer or omit.

A human-critical unit is one whose omission could change judgment of outcome/scope, gate, decision, contract, delivery, proof, risk/recovery, freshness or next action. Its decision-relevant meaning must remain visible with provenance; a bare pointer is insufficient. Governed formats preserve required obligations. Candidate comparisons use a common scale and retain supplied dispositions; exact evidence remains retrievable.

Preserve verdict, confidence, score, gate, readiness, evidence gap and epistemic status as distinct types. Do not average away a hard gate or infer a conclusion from presentation.

Use progressive disclosure: current judgment and blockers visible; rationale, alternatives and detailed proof one action away; exhaustive logs/packets at canonical sources. Blocking, stale or unproved material never hides in disclosure. For substantial artifacts, embed only a compact context capsule of identity/revision, purpose, status, blocker/next action, high-value locators, source cut and proof freshness.

For living projections, foreground material delta—changed decisions, stale claims, new evidence, closed proof, blockers and next action—while retaining quieter context. Update source truth first, then the view; keep planned, implemented, reviewed, tested and live-verified states separate.

Maintain an internal coverage map from each critical obligation to source identity, visible placement, provenance and present/partial/stale/missing state. Expose gaps rather than inventing closure. Before delivery check coherent source cut, critical coverage, opening/current action, retrieval, faithfulness, counterevidence, context hygiene and proof fit. Reuse still-valid browser evidence and rerun only invalidated checks.
