# Technical explanation

Use when supplied technical material needs a bounded explanation that a reader can understand and act on. A substantial unanswered factual question uses Investigation first.

1. Establish the reader's question, supplied evidence, desired depth, and action the explanation should enable. Reopen research only for a material source gap.
2. Use [oro-eniyan](../commands/oro-eniyan.md) to preserve exact identifiers, facts, decisions, authority, and the artifact's native contract.
3. Use [fihanmi](../commands/fihanmi.md) for the smallest useful structural view and [html-artifact](../commands/html-artifact.md) only when a portable inspectable presentation materially improves comprehension.
4. Check the explanation against the supplied mechanism and reader job. Keep uncertainty and unverified claims visible.

Finish with the usable explanation or requested artifact. Do not turn explanation into implementation, documentation reconciliation, or publication without that requested outcome.
