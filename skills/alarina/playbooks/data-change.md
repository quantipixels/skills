# Data change

Use when a migration, backfill or rollout must preserve populated data, compatibility or recoverability. Honor a planning-only or read-only audit request.

1. Recover the governing behavior, actual candidate and existing plan. Route unresolved domain meaning to `amose`, observable behavior to `atona` in behavior-contract mode, and consequential storage or coexistence design to `architect`. Reuse settled decisions.
2. `alaga` establishes the required existing-data mapping and stateful proof from authorized representative data. Its result feeds acceptance; `atunwo` can independently assess the candidate and evidence gaps. Resolve migration or measurement setup through the project's existing capability.
3. For consequential rollout or recovery, `atona` incorporates concrete checks, stop conditions and recovery obligations into the current plan using its rollout-readiness method. Preserve the distinction between code rollback, data recovery and irreversible effects.
4. Stop here for planning-only work. With delivery authority, `alaga` implements and verifies the accepted change, including relevant intermediate and retry behavior. Live rollout belongs to the project's authorized execution capability; do not infer that authority from a ready plan or open PR.
5. Return execution evidence to the same plan. A failed mapping, uncertain recovery or unavailable real boundary remains unresolved; reroute that specific gap before dependent progression.

Finish at the requested audit, plan, verified implementation or authorized rollout outcome. A schema diff, backup or passing empty-database test is not proof that existing meaning survived.
