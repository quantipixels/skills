# Model workflows

Each workflow splits a job so cheap models do the wide or long work and expensive models do the deciding. Models are row keys from [models](models.md), so overrides there apply here; when a step's row is the lead's own model, the lead does that step itself unless the models page says to delegate it. Use a workflow only when the work is big enough to pay for the hand-offs; a small job stays with one agent.

| # | Workflow | Steps | Models and effort | Skip when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reader → judge | A reader writes a positioned record ([reader records](reader-records.md)); the judge works from it, spot-checking named gaps | Reader: `cheap`. Judge: the owner's row (`judgment` for plans, `build` for builds) | The source is short, or exact reading matters (security, money, legal) |
| 2 | Explore → explain | 2–4 read-only explorers each trace one slice of a system; one explainer writes the explanation for the reader | Explorers: `cheap`. Explainer: `writing` | One file or one flow explains it |
| 3 | Investigate → synthesize | One investigator per evidence source (code history, PRs, issues, docs, chat); a synthesizer separates facts, inferences, competing explanations and gaps | Investigators: `cheap` (Sol medium for deep code-history tracing). Synthesizer: `judgment` | The answer sits in one source |
| 4 | Race → cross-judge | Candidates solve one brief in separate paths; the judge's rubric (3–6 criteria) is set first and not shown to them; the judge scores against it; the lead reads all and grafts strong parts into one result | Candidates: `race` (Sol xhigh and Opus low), both delegated. Judge: `validator`, which the lead does when it is Opus; it fixes the rubric before the candidates start and writes no candidate | The shape is settled or the work is routine |
| 5 | Two-shape design | Two structurally different designs from different providers, each starting from how callers use it; pick or combine, then fix the contract | Designers: `design` and Sol xhigh (`design-new` replaces `design` for ground-breaking work). Pick: the lead, or `independent` when the lead designed one | One obvious shape exists |
| 6 | Panel review | The same packet and rubric go to two reviewers; dedupe; record agreement and disagreement; one proven correctness or security defect is never outvoted | Reviewers: Sol xhigh and `judgment`; when one reviewer is the author's own model, use `independent` instead. Dedupe: `validator` | The change is small, or the lead is the expected reviewer |
| 7 | Lens fan-out → validate | Cheap scanners each check one risk (security, data, concurrency, tests, performance) and flag candidates with positions; an expensive model validates only the flags | Scanners: `cheap`. Validator: `validator`, or `independent` when the lead's model wrote the change | The diff is small enough to read whole |
| 8 | Freeze, then peer | The lead writes its own judgment first, then reads one peer from the other provider and reconciles; agreement alone is not proof | Peer: `independent` (Sol xhigh for an Opus lead; Opus medium for a Sol lead) | The decision is easy to reverse |
| 9 | Gauntlet (swarm) | Many independent checks fanned out to cheap workers; every slice reports evidence; a missing slice is a gap, not a pass | Workers: `cheap` (`cheap-browser` for browser checks). Aggregator: the lead | Fewer checks than workers would cost |
| 10 | Hillclimb | Per round: one hypothesis, one change, one measurement; keep or revert behind a regression gate; stop at the target or when gains flatten | Changer: `build`. Harness runner: `cheap`. Verdict: the lead with `iwon`'s rules | No reliable measurement exists |
| 11 | Draft → critique → revise | For writing that matters (PR bodies, specs, docs for people): draft, an independent critique against the reader's needs, revise | Drafter and reviser: `writing`. Critic: `validator`, or `independent` when the lead drafted | Routine or short text |
| 12 | Reflection lenses | A reader records the session; separate lenses for judgment, tooling and blind spots; one synthesis into Accepted, Rejected and Backlog | Reader: `cheap`. Tooling lens: Sol medium. Judgment lens: `judgment`. Blind-spot lens: `independent`, since the lead cannot see its own blind spots. Synthesis: the lead | A short session; one judge suffices |
| 13 | Scripted runs | Exact, checkable steps run by a cheap runner while the lead keeps thinking: test suites, builds, lints, formatters, a verify skill's launch-drive-record recipe (including driving a browser with `preview_*`), screenshots, log and data pulls. The runner follows the steps exactly, stops at the first unexpected state, changes nothing outside the recipe, and returns the evidence (commands, result lines, files) | Runner: `cheap`, or `cheap-browser` for browser drives and screenshots. Judge of the evidence: the lead | The steps need judgment mid-way, or the run is one quick command |

Hand-offs carry the record or result and the decisions so far, never the whole conversation. Every child follows the brief rules (checkpoints, verified paths, early first checkpoint) and the lead checks results itself.
