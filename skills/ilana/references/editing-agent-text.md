# Editing agent text

Use for material review, revision or pruning of agent-facing prose. Local wording and pointer corrections can be handled directly. Use [instruction economics](instruction-economics.md) when pruning or changing attention and disclosure.

## Map and evolve the contract

Pin exact identifiers, schemas, accepted decisions and authority. Trace the governed path through entrypoint, references/contracts, templates/tools, workflow consumer and handoff. Identify:

- triggers, exclusions, branches and skip conditions;
- actions, side effects and authority;
- required outcomes/boundaries, conditional requirements, defaults and examples;
- completion bounds and evidence;
- required or useful output formats and how the reader answers;
- compatibility, fallback and recovery; and
- pointers and load conditions.

Classify each material semantic part as **retain**, **strengthen**, **relocate**, **replace** or **retire**. The baseline exposes accidental loss; it does not make old wording authoritative. Keep live constraints because they affect useful behaviour, not because they existed before. Retire an expired compatibility rule, but preserve a live route that prevents an adjacent-owner collision.

A wording-only edit preserves semantics. A refactor can change them within scope and evidence. A template's example fields or construction choices do not become mandatory by implication. Where substitution is permitted, check that the alternative fits and preserves required meaning, authority, compatibility, fallback and proof. Refresh affected evidence.

## Verify the changed boundary

Compare with intended current behaviour:

1. Are triggers, branches and owner boundaries intentional and reachable?
2. Did authority or side effects broaden unintentionally?
3. Are material bounds clear and demanding, with required proof still open when missing?
4. Can the reader distinguish binding/conditional rules from defaults/examples and judge permitted alternatives?
5. Do moved references retain useful load conditions?
6. Were compatibility, fallback and recovery deliberately retained, replaced or retired?
7. Can the reader still recognise, compare and answer the output, with useful formats and examples intact or deliberately replaced?
8. Did pruning remove unnecessary load rather than merely shorten sentences?

Use realistic before/after tasks when selection, authority, completion, routing or execution changes materially. Editorial cleanup needs no evaluation harness. Package checks prove structure; execution, installation, discovery and user-visible success need their own evidence when claimed.

Return the revised text or findings, material retired/replaced/relocated semantics and proof gaps. Keep that explanation in the task's review or handoff; create no permanent preservation dossier.
