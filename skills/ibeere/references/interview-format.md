# Interview format

## Question card

Render as Markdown, not as a template fence. Keep question numbers stable across rounds; label choices when that makes answers unambiguous.

```text
❓ **Q1** — **<question title>**: <question and choices, as needed>

💡 <brief context, evidence, trade-off or example, only when it makes the decision clearer>

➡️ <recommended answer and why>
```

## Rubric for close alternatives

Use a compact, decision-specific rubric when a frontier choice has several credible alternatives and at least two independent criteria can change the recommendation. Apply hard gates first; use only criteria that matter here; show the decisive factor, the strongest alternative and material counterevidence. Do not score a trivial or already-determined choice, and do not let an average hide a gate. The rubric supports the recommendation; the decision stays the user's.
