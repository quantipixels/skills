# Triggered principle index

Apply only principles whose trigger is present. Load the named leaf or owner record when its full contract is needed; this index is not a substitute for that source.

| Trigger | Principle | Leaf reference | Required decision evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Several routes appear plausible | Choose the smallest useful route with one primary outcome owner | `alarina` | Selected owner, necessary support, and rejected overlap |
| A reversible observation or prototype can resolve the fork | Obtain empirical evidence before asking an avoidable question | use `alarina` to resolve the available investigation owner; otherwise keep the observation with the active owner | Observation, result, and remaining material choice |
| Terms, state, invariants, or ownership are unstable | Model the domain before stateful work | `amose` | Exact terms, relationships, invariants, and destination |
| New machinery is proposed | Subtract, reuse, or deepen an existing owner before adding another surface | `pare` | Existing seam, rejected smaller change, and justified addition |
| A failure invites a local workaround | Repair the earliest credible owning cause | `se-triage`, then the selected delivery owner | Reproduction or mechanism, owner, repair, and regression proof |
| A proposal or tool attempt is being treated as success | Prove the real artifact or external state | active leaf owner | Current readback tied to acceptance |
| Work has multiple dependent steps | Sequence independently verifiable units and expose the next startable unit | `seda-ticket` when decomposition is the outcome | Unit boundaries, dependencies, proof, and throughput checkpoint |
| A controller could absorb specialist work | Preserve outcome ownership and native authority gates | `alarina` and the active leaf | Controller responsibility, leaf responsibility, and gated actions |
| A lesson recurs | Encode it in the strongest owning structure before adding prose | structural owner, `amose`, or `ko-skill` | Recurrence evidence, destination, owner receipt, and verification |
| Work must stop or cross a context boundary | Pause at a safe checkpoint that a fresh agent can verify | `handoff` | Candidate identity, completed proof, open gap, authority, and first action |

Do not apply a principle merely because its wording sounds generally useful. Record the trigger and the evidence it changed before letting it alter the task route or phase.
