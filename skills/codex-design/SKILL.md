# Codex Design

Interrupt the default composition before it becomes code. Apply this steering during the existing design task; keep implementation, content authority and delivery with their current owners.

## Before the first build

Read the actual brief, content, existing design and available assets. Identify what the visitor should see or do first. An explicit aesthetic or accepted design wins over the corrections below.

Choose a subject-specific composition before reaching for components. If the first idea is a centered slogan followed by equal cards, or a sidebar/dashboard for an explanation, consider a structurally different arrangement. Pick the one that serves the content; do not generate a disposable first version merely to reject it.

Make one decisive visual move carry the surface: the work or product itself, a revealing comparison, a mechanism, or a clear action. Commit through hierarchy, scale, density and space together. A font swap and accent color are not enough. Keep this choice concise and within the existing plan; no extra design ritual or approval round.

## Correct the reflex, not the brief

- **Default shell:** do not turn every section into an icon, label and rounded card. Use alignment and grouping; keep cards for independent objects or actions. Repetition earns its place for comparable items.
- **Decorative sophistication:** omit gratuitous badges, micro-labels, numbered headings, technical grids and status dots. Retain them when they communicate real identity, order, scale or state.
- **Type as spectacle:** fit real copy to its reading task and viewport. Avoid oversized tightly tracked headings that crowd out useful content; do not enforce universal line counts, fonts or spacing quotas.
- **Safe cosmetic variation:** change the composition when it is interchangeable, rather than cycling through beige editorial, dark neon or purple gradients. Each can be right when the brief supports it.
- **Placeholder substance:** use the actual imagery, example, data or relationship. Do not manufacture metrics, testimonials, decorative code or pseudo-diagrams to fill the layout. If a needed asset is absent, keep the limitation honest.
- **Effects as proof of effort:** animation must earn its role in the experience. Preserve readable static content, reduced motion, familiar controls and keyboard/touch use. Neither mandatory motion nor mandatory austerity is the cure.

Use the current project stack. No randomisation script, compulsory component library, font blacklist or animation dependency is required by this skill. Do not simulate tool output or claim layout/contrast guarantees before inspecting the result.

## Check the delivered surface

Use the existing delivery owner's render evidence to check the chosen composition with real content at the relevant widths. Look for the original default returning during implementation: repeated containers, arbitrary ornament, flattened emphasis or useful content pushed out of view. Correct the specific regression within scope.

Stop when the requested surface expresses its subject and remains usable. Report unverified appearance when rendering is unavailable; your design judgment is not measured user preference. For an HTML information artifact, `html-artifact` retains construction, source fidelity and living-report behavior.
